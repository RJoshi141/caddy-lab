# Trial close

> MOCK DATA. Written for the Caddy Lab prototype to stand in for a real recruiting best-practice corpus.

## What's really going on

A trial close checks commitment without asking for the final yes. Use it after every major objection is handled, so you're never surprised at the offer stage.

## Dig deeper

1. If they came back with that number, would you accept it?
2. Is there anyone else who needs to be comfortable with this before you say yes?
3. What would you say to them if they called you tomorrow with an offer?

## Watch for

Hesitation on a trial close is gold. It means there's an objection left, and you found it early.
