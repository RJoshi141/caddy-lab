# Salary history

> MOCK DATA. Written for the Caddy Lab prototype to stand in for a real recruiting best-practice corpus.

## What's really going on

Asking for current salary is legally restricted in several places, and candidates increasingly push back on it. Ask for expectations instead, and anchor on the market, not their history.

## Dig deeper

1. What are you looking for in your next package, rather than what you're on now?
2. Where did that expectation come from, the market, a friend, another offer?
3. What's the lowest number that would still feel like progress?

## Watch for

Check local rules before asking about current pay at all.
