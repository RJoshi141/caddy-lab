# Motivation mismatch

> MOCK DATA. Written for the Caddy Lab prototype to stand in for a real recruiting best-practice corpus.

## What's really going on

Lukewarm language is the loudest signal on a call and the easiest to miss. 'I guess', 'it's fine', 'could be OK' all mean the role doesn't connect to what they actually want. Don't sell harder. Selling a lukewarm candidate gets you an accept followed by a no-show or a three month leaver. Find out what would make them excited, then honestly decide whether this role does it. Sometimes the right move is to park them and come back with the right job. That trust pays off later.

## Dig deeper

1. When you picture your next role going really well, what are you doing on a Tuesday afternoon?
2. What part of this role, if any, actually got your attention?
3. What would have to be true about this job for you to be genuinely excited, not just OK with it?
4. If this isn't it, what should I be looking for on your behalf?

## Watch for

If their answer to the first question describes a different job, say so. You'll be the first recruiter who has.
