# Employer brand concerns

> MOCK DATA. Written for the Caddy Lab prototype to stand in for a real recruiting best-practice corpus.

## What's really going on

Glassdoor reviews and word of mouth shape decisions more than anything the hiring manager says. Ask what they read. Often it's two angry reviews from a team they wouldn't even be on.

## Dig deeper

1. What specifically did you read, and which team was it about?
2. Do you know anyone who works there, or used to?
3. Would it help to speak to someone on the actual team?

## Watch for

Offering a peer conversation is the fastest way to cut through bad reviews.
