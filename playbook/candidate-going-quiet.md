# Candidate going quiet

> MOCK DATA. Written for the Caddy Lab prototype to stand in for a real recruiting best-practice corpus.

## What's really going on

When a candidate stops replying, it's rarely because they're busy. They're usually talking to someone else, having doubts, or embarrassed about saying no. Make it easy for them to tell you the truth.

## Dig deeper

1. Has anything changed since we last spoke?
2. Is there something about the role you've been unsure how to bring up?
3. Would it be easier if I just asked: are you still interested?

## Watch for

Give people an easy out. The ones who take it were going to leave anyway.
