# Equity skepticism

> MOCK DATA. Written for the Caddy Lab prototype to stand in for a real recruiting best-practice corpus.

## What's really going on

Plenty of candidates have been burned by options that went to zero. When they say 'equity is just paper', they're telling you about their last company, not this one. Don't argue the upside. Ask what would make it feel real. The answer tells you whether you're dealing with risk aversion, a cash flow need, or a story they've heard. Cash flow needs are solvable with base. Risk aversion is a fit question.

## Dig deeper

1. Was there a past company where equity didn't work out for you? What happened?
2. If you knew the equity was worth zero, would the base on its own still work?
3. What would you need to know about the company to start counting it?

## Watch for

A candidate with a mortgage and young kids saying this is usually a base problem dressed up as an equity problem.
