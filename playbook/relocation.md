# Relocation

> MOCK DATA. Written for the Caddy Lab prototype to stand in for a real recruiting best-practice corpus.

## What's really going on

Relocation objections are rarely about the move itself. They're about a partner's job, schools, or an ageing parent. Ask about the people, not the logistics.

## Dig deeper

1. Who else does this move affect, and how do they feel about it?
2. What would a realistic timeline for moving look like for you?
3. Would a relocation package or a remote start period make a difference?

## Watch for

If the partner hasn't been asked yet, the candidate hasn't really decided.
