# Role scope

> MOCK DATA. Written for the Caddy Lab prototype to stand in for a real recruiting best-practice corpus.

## What's really going on

Vague scope is a deal killer. Candidates who aren't clear what they'd own will fill the gap with their worst fear. Get specific: what they'd own on day one, at six months, and what success looks like at a year.

## Dig deeper

1. What would you want to own on day one for this to feel like a real job?
2. What's the part of the scope that's still fuzzy for you?
3. If you could ask the hiring manager one question about the role, what would it be?

## Watch for

If the client can't answer the scope questions, that's a client problem. Raise it.
