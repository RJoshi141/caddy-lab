# Base vs total comp

> MOCK DATA. Written for the Caddy Lab prototype to stand in for a real recruiting best-practice corpus.

## What's really going on

Candidates compare base salary to base salary because it's the only number that's easy to hold in your head. Bonus, equity, pension contribution and benefits get waved away as 'nice to have'. Often the offer is genuinely competitive on total and weak on base, and the candidate hasn't clocked it. Sometimes the opposite: their current bonus is mostly theoretical and they're overvaluing it. Before anyone negotiates, get both packages written down side by side. You're not trying to talk them out of their concern. You're making sure the concern is real.

## Dig deeper

1. What did your bonus actually pay out the last two years, not the target, the real number?
2. If we lined both packages up on one page, which line would you want to see move first?
3. How much of the equity are you counting as real money today?

## Watch for

If they light up about equity, check they understand vesting and the strike price. Excitement now becomes regret at the offer stage.
