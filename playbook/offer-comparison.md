# Offer comparison

> MOCK DATA. Written for the Caddy Lab prototype to stand in for a real recruiting best-practice corpus.

## What's really going on

Candidates comparing two offers tend to fixate on the biggest number and the most recent conversation. Help them build a fair comparison: money, growth, manager, commute, stability. You're not stacking the deck. If your role loses on a fair comparison, you want to know now rather than after they've resigned and then accepted a counter.

## Dig deeper

1. If you scored both on manager, growth and money, where does each one win?
2. Which of those three matters most to you a year from now?
3. Who else are you talking this decision through with?

## Watch for

If they keep talking about the other manager, that's your signal. Money won't fix a people gap.
