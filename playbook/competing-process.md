# Competing process

> MOCK DATA. Written for the Caddy Lab prototype to stand in for a real recruiting best-practice corpus.

## What's really going on

When a candidate mentions another process, they're usually doing one of three things: genuinely telling you the timeline, creating urgency to get a better number, or quietly preparing you for a no. You need to know which. Don't panic and don't get competitive about the other company. Get curious. The single most valuable fact is what they like about the other role that this one doesn't have. That's the real objection, and it's often not money at all.

## Dig deeper

1. Where are you with them, honestly, and what's the next step and date?
2. What do they have that this role doesn't, if you had to name one thing?
3. If both offers landed on the same day with the same number, which would you pick and why?
4. What would this client need to do this week to stay in the running?

## Watch for

If they can't name anything the other role does better, the other process is leverage, not a threat. Handle it as a comp conversation.
