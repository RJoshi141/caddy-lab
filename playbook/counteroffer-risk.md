# Counteroffer risk

> MOCK DATA. Written for the Caddy Lab prototype to stand in for a real recruiting best-practice corpus.

## What's really going on

Counteroffers are the most common way an accepted offer dies. If the candidate mentions their manager would 'probably match', assume they will, and assume the candidate is half considering it. The research every recruiter quotes is that most people who accept a counter leave within a year anyway, but quoting that sounds self-serving. Instead, get them to say out loud why they started looking. If money alone fixes that reason, you have a real risk.

## Dig deeper

1. What made you start looking in the first place? Go back to the very first moment.
2. If they matched the money tomorrow, would that reason go away?
3. How do you think your manager would react when you hand your notice in?
4. What would you say to them if they offered a promotion to stay?

## Watch for

Rehearse the resignation conversation before the offer is signed, not after.
