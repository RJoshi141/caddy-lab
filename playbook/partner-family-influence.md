# Partner and family influence

> MOCK DATA. Written for the Caddy Lab prototype to stand in for a real recruiting best-practice corpus.

## What's really going on

Big career decisions get made at the kitchen table, not on the call. If the candidate keeps saying 'we', there's someone else in the decision. Find out who, and what they think.

## Dig deeper

1. Who else are you talking this decision through with?
2. What's their take so far?
3. What would they need to hear to feel good about it?

## Watch for

A partner who hasn't been consulted is a late-stage decline waiting to happen.
