# Hiring manager fit

> MOCK DATA. Written for the Caddy Lab prototype to stand in for a real recruiting best-practice corpus.

## What's really going on

People join companies and leave managers. If a candidate didn't click with the hiring manager, that's the most important thing they'll say on the call. Find out whether it was style, a bad day, or a values clash. A bad day can be fixed with a second conversation. A values clash can't.

## Dig deeper

1. What specifically didn't land, was it something they said or how they said it?
2. What's the best manager you've had like, compared to them?
3. Would a second, more informal conversation with them help?
4. If you had a different manager in the same role, would you take it?

## Watch for

If they'd take the role with a different manager, the role is fine and the manager isn't. Tell the client gently.
