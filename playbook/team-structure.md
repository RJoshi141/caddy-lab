# Team structure

> MOCK DATA. Written for the Caddy Lab prototype to stand in for a real recruiting best-practice corpus.

## What's really going on

Who they'd work with and who they'd report to matters more than the job description. Get the team size, who's senior, and whether they'd be the only one doing what they do.

## Dig deeper

1. Who would you be working with day to day, do you know yet?
2. Would you be the only person doing this, or part of a group?
3. What size team do you do your best work in?

## Watch for

Being the only specialist on a team is exciting for some people and lonely for others. Ask which.
