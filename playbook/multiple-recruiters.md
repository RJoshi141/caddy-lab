# Multiple recruiters

> MOCK DATA. Written for the Caddy Lab prototype to stand in for a real recruiting best-practice corpus.

## What's really going on

Candidates working with several agencies often get submitted twice or lose track of where they are. Ask directly, and be the recruiter who's organised.

## Dig deeper

1. Who else are you working with, and where have they put you forward?
2. Has anyone submitted you to this client already?
3. Would it help if we lined up all your processes in one place?

## Watch for

Double submissions embarrass everyone. Check before you send the CV.
