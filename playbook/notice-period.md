# Notice period

> MOCK DATA. Written for the Caddy Lab prototype to stand in for a real recruiting best-practice corpus.

## What's really going on

Long notice periods scare clients more than candidates. Get the contractual notice, what's realistic to negotiate down, and whether garden leave is likely. A candidate who has never checked their contract is a candidate who hasn't really pictured leaving.

## Dig deeper

1. What does your contract actually say, have you looked at it recently?
2. Have people at your company negotiated an earlier release before?
3. If you were on garden leave, would you be able to start anything early?

## Watch for

Three months' notice plus a counteroffer is the riskiest combination you'll see. Stay in touch weekly during notice.
