# Commute

> MOCK DATA. Written for the Caddy Lab prototype to stand in for a real recruiting best-practice corpus.

## What's really going on

Candidates underestimate commutes at interview stage and overestimate them at offer stage. Get the real door to door time and how often.

## Dig deeper

1. Have you actually done the journey at rush hour yet?
2. How many days a week would you be making that trip?
3. What's the longest commute you've done before, and how did it go?

## Watch for

An hour each way, three days a week, is a common quiet reason for declining.
