# Passive candidate

> MOCK DATA. Written for the Caddy Lab prototype to stand in for a real recruiting best-practice corpus.

## What's really going on

Passive candidates aren't against moving, they just haven't found a reason. The mistake is pitching features of the job. The better move is finding the itch: the thing about their current job that annoys them a little more each month. That itch is your opening, and it's almost never about money.

## Dig deeper

1. What's one thing about your current job you'd change if you could?
2. How long has that been bugging you?
3. What would have to show up for you to take a call like this seriously?

## Watch for

If there's genuinely no itch, stay in touch and move on. Pushing burns the relationship.
