# Exploding offer

> MOCK DATA. Written for the Caddy Lab prototype to stand in for a real recruiting best-practice corpus.

## What's really going on

An offer with a hard deadline is a pressure tactic, and candidates know it, but it still works on them. Your job is to slow the decision down without looking like you're stalling for your client. Find out whether the deadline is real, whether they've asked for more time, and how they actually feel about the other company once you take the clock away.

## Dig deeper

1. Is that deadline firm, or have you asked them for more time yet?
2. If the deadline wasn't there, would you take that offer today?
3. What would you want to have seen from us before Friday to feel good either way?

## Watch for

Candidates who'd say yes without the deadline are gone. Spend your energy on the ones who hesitate.
