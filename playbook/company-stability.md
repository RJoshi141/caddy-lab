# Company stability

> MOCK DATA. Written for the Caddy Lab prototype to stand in for a real recruiting best-practice corpus.

## What's really going on

Since the layoffs of the last few years, candidates ask about runway and stability far more than they used to. Don't reassure them with fluff. Find out what they've read or heard, and get the client to answer directly. A client that won't talk about runway is telling you something too.

## Dig deeper

1. What have you heard, and where did you hear it?
2. What would you need to know to feel comfortable, runway, revenue, investors?
3. Would a conversation with their finance lead or founder help?

## Watch for

Candidates with a family and a mortgage weigh stability more than they say out loud.
