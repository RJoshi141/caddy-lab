# Bonus or vesting cliff

> MOCK DATA. Written for the Caddy Lab prototype to stand in for a real recruiting best-practice corpus.

## What's really going on

Candidates close to a bonus payout or vesting cliff will quietly drag their feet. It's rational. Get the date and the amount on the table early, so the client can decide whether to buy it out or wait. Hiding it until offer stage kills deals.

## Dig deeper

1. When does your bonus pay out, and roughly how much are we talking?
2. Is there any equity vesting in the next six months?
3. If the client covered that, or timed your start after it, does the timing worry go away?

## Watch for

Buyouts are more common than candidates think. Ask the client before assuming no.
