# Level calibration

> MOCK DATA. Written for the Caddy Lab prototype to stand in for a real recruiting best-practice corpus.

## What's really going on

When a candidate thinks they're being pitched below their level, they're often right. Interview loops tend to down-level. Get specifics: what level they're at now, what scope that means, and what the client's ladder actually looks like.

## Dig deeper

1. What does your current level mean in practice, team size, decisions you own?
2. Which part of the interview made you feel it was pitched lower?
3. If the client re-levelled you after six months, would that work?

## Watch for

A promise of re-levelling is only worth something in writing.
