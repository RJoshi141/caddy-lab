# Remote and hybrid

> MOCK DATA. Written for the Caddy Lab prototype to stand in for a real recruiting best-practice corpus.

## What's really going on

Office days are now one of the top three reasons offers get declined. When a candidate pushes back on days in the office, find out whether it's about time, family logistics, or principle. Each has a different fix. Time problems can sometimes be solved with flexible hours. Family logistics need specifics. Principle usually can't be moved.

## Dig deeper

1. Is it the number of days, or which days, or the commute itself?
2. What does your week look like right now, and what would break?
3. If it were two days with flexible hours, would that change things?

## Watch for

Check what the client will actually flex on before you float anything.
