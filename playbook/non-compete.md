# Non-compete

> MOCK DATA. Written for the Caddy Lab prototype to stand in for a real recruiting best-practice corpus.

## What's really going on

Non-competes are often less enforceable than candidates fear, but you're not a lawyer and shouldn't act like one. Get the clause in writing and let the client's legal team assess it. Your job is to find out how worried the candidate is and whether it's actually blocking them.

## Dig deeper

1. Do you have a copy of the clause itself, or is this from memory?
2. Has anyone from your company been held to it before?
3. If legal said it's fine, would you be ready to go?

## Watch for

Candidates sometimes use the non-compete as a reason not to decide. Check if it's the real blocker.
