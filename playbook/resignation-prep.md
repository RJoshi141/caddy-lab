# Resignation prep

> MOCK DATA. Written for the Caddy Lab prototype to stand in for a real recruiting best-practice corpus.

## What's really going on

The resignation meeting is where deals fall apart. Candidates walk in nervous, get love-bombed, and walk out undecided. Prep it like an interview. Agree the words they'll use, agree they won't negotiate in the room, and agree when they'll call you afterwards.

## Dig deeper

1. When are you planning to tell your manager, and what will you actually say?
2. If they ask what it would take to keep you, what's your answer?
3. Can you call me straight after, even if it's just two minutes?

## Watch for

A candidate who won't commit to a resignation date is not resigning.
