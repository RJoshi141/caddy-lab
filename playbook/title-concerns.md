# Title concerns

> MOCK DATA. Written for the Caddy Lab prototype to stand in for a real recruiting best-practice corpus.

## What's really going on

Title objections come from three places: how it looks on LinkedIn, how it affects the next job search, and pride. All three are valid. Find out which, then check what the title actually means at this client. A 'Senior' at a 40 person startup can be broader than a 'Lead' at a bank. Sometimes the client will flex the title for free, it costs them nothing.

## Dig deeper

1. Is it how it looks externally, or what it says about your scope?
2. What title would you need to see, and why that one?
3. If the scope and pay were right, how much would the title still matter?
4. Would it help if I asked whether the title is flexible?

## Watch for

Title is one of the cheapest things for a client to give. Ask early.
