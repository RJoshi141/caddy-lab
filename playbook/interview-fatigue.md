# Interview fatigue

> MOCK DATA. Written for the Caddy Lab prototype to stand in for a real recruiting best-practice corpus.

## What's really going on

Long processes kill momentum. After the fourth round, even keen candidates start wondering if the company knows what it wants. Acknowledge it, and push the client for a decision date.

## Dig deeper

1. How are you feeling about the process so far, honestly?
2. Is the length of it putting you off, or just tiring?
3. If I could get a decision this week, would that change anything?

## Watch for

Fatigue plus a competing offer is the classic way a strong candidate slips away.
