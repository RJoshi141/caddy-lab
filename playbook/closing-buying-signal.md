# Buying signal

> MOCK DATA. Written for the Caddy Lab prototype to stand in for a real recruiting best-practice corpus.

## What's really going on

When a candidate says 'that sounds great' or asks about next steps, stop selling. Most recruiters keep talking and talk themselves out of the deal. Confirm the interest, surface any last objection, and lock the next step with a date.

## Dig deeper

1. On a scale of one to ten, where are you right now, and what would make it a ten?
2. Is there anything that would stop you saying yes if an offer came through?
3. Can we lock in the next conversation before we hang up?

## Watch for

A six or seven is a real answer. Ask what the missing points are, that's your remaining objection.
