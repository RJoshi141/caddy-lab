# Client going quiet

> MOCK DATA. Written for the Caddy Lab prototype to stand in for a real recruiting best-practice corpus.

## What's really going on

When the client or hiring manager goes quiet, candidates assume the worst and start entertaining other offers. Be honest about what you know and what you don't. Give them a date when you'll update them, even if the update is 'still nothing'.

## Dig deeper

1. What have you been telling yourself about the silence?
2. Are other processes moving while you wait?
3. If I can get you an answer by Thursday, will you hold until then?

## Watch for

Silence from the client for more than a week is a deal risk. Chase internally before the candidate chases you.
