# Career direction

> MOCK DATA. Written for the Caddy Lab prototype to stand in for a real recruiting best-practice corpus.

## What's really going on

When a candidate talks about where they want to be in three years, listen for whether this role is a step on that path or a detour. People will take a detour for money, but they don't stay. Map the path out loud with them and be honest about where this role sits.

## Dig deeper

1. Where do you want to be in three years, and what's the step that gets you there?
2. Does this role move you closer to that, or is it a side step?
3. What would you need to learn in the next job to make that jump?

## Watch for

Candidates who want leadership but are being pitched an IC role are a classic mismatch. Name it early.
