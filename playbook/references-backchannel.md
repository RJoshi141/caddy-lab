# References and backchannel

> MOCK DATA. Written for the Caddy Lab prototype to stand in for a real recruiting best-practice corpus.

## What's really going on

Candidates worry about backchannel references more than they admit, especially in small industries. If they get nervous at reference stage, ask why.

## Dig deeper

1. Who would you want them to speak to, and who would you rather they didn't?
2. Is there anything a former manager might say that you'd want to get ahead of?
3. Would it help to brief your references before they get the call?

## Watch for

Nervousness at reference stage is often about one specific person. Find out who.
